Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Introduction

Myōko Massage uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our Website in compliance with the GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC), and Greek Law 4624/2019. This policy explains how we use cookies, what information they collect, and your choices regarding their use.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They serve different purposes:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary files deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted
  • First-Party Cookies: Set directly by our Website
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by external services we use

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Consent Required: No — These are exempt from consent requirements under GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

2.2 Functional Cookies

Consent Required: Yes — These require your explicit opt-in consent.

These enhance experience by remembering preferences such as language and theme selection. Duration: 1 year.

2.3 Analytics Cookies

Consent Required: Yes — These require explicit opt-in consent and are not loaded until consent is granted.

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our Website — such as pages visited, session duration, and device type. This data is anonymised and aggregated; it is not used to personally identify you. Google Analytics cookies are only placed on your device after you accept analytics cookies via our cookie banner.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by withdrawing your consent via our cookie banner. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

2.4 Marketing & Advertising Cookies

Consent Required: Yes — These require explicit opt-in consent.

We do not use marketing or advertising cookies. We do not track you across other websites or serve targeted advertisements.

3. Consent & Opt-In Requirements

  • Non-essential cookies require your prior explicit, informed, and freely given consent
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) are not loaded by default — they are only initialised after you accept via the cookie banner
  • You have granular control — accept or reject cookies by category
  • You can withdraw consent at any time with the same ease as providing it
  • We do not deny Website access if you refuse non-essential cookies
  • All consent checkboxes are unchecked by default — no pre-ticking

4.3 Instagram

Links to our Instagram account are provided for your convenience. Visiting Instagram may result in Instagram setting their own cookies on your device. Please refer to Instagram's Data Policy for details.

4.4 No Other Third-Party Trackers

We do not embed any other third-party tracking pixels, Hotjar, Facebook Pixel, or similar tools. Any future additions will be disclosed in this policy and will require your prior explicit consent.

5. Cookie Duration

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: 1 year from the date of creation (except _ga which lasts 2 years)
  • Consent Preference Cookie: 1 year

6. How to Control & Delete Cookies

6.1 Browser Settings

  • Chrome: Settings › Privacy and Security › Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Preferences › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences › Privacy › Cookies and Website Data
  • Edge: Settings › Privacy, search, and services › Cookies and other site data

6.2 Google Analytics Opt-Out

You can specifically opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by withdrawing analytics consent via our cookie banner at any time.

6.3 Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw consent at any time by revisiting the cookie banner, clearing your browser cookies, or contacting us at hello@myokomassage.com.

6.4 Do-Not-Track (DNT)

If you enable Do-Not-Track signals in your browser, our Website will respect your preference and limit cookies to essential only.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

  • Right to Information: Receive clear, transparent information about cookies used
  • Right to Consent: Give or withhold consent before non-essential cookies are placed
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Change your mind at any time with ease
  • Right to Object: Request we stop using specific cookies
  • Right to Access: Request full information about cookies we use

8. Children's Privacy

Our Website is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly allow children to consent to cookies or provide personal data. Parents or guardians concerned about a child's privacy should contact us immediately.
