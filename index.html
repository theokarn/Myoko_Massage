About Myōko

We bring the art of therapeutic massage to your doorstep in Mykonos. Whether you're at your villa, hotel, or beachside retreat, our mobile service delivers personalised relaxation and recovery treatments designed for your body's unique needs.

Trained in Swedish, deep tissue, and sports massage techniques, we combine ancient wellness wisdom with modern therapeutic practice.
